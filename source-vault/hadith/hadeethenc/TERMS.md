# HadeethEnc source-use record

Source: https://hadeethenc.com/en  
Checked: 2026-09-25

HadeethEnc states that its translated Hadith content may be downloaded and republished subject to
conditions. Aaris preserves the downloaded wording without modification, identifies HadeethEnc.com
as publisher/source, records the translation version, keeps the source metadata available, and
treats newer official versions as replacements for the active snapshot. Translation observations
should be reported to HadeethEnc, and the content must not be presented alongside inappropriate
advertising.

This file is a provenance note, not a substitute for the publisher's current terms. The exact
official source URLs and versioned download URLs are recorded in
`current/manifest.json`. If the upstream terms materially change, source admission must be
reviewed before a new snapshot is activated.
