# Product North Star

> Read → get stuck → tap → understand → keep reading.

The default surface is the Quran text, not a dashboard. A first tap reveals only enough contextual meaning to continue reading. Expansion may reveal morphology, root/lemma, grammar, audio, occurrence relationships and learning actions while keeping the ayah anchored.

Source-faithful Quran/Hadith content is immutable and traceable. Search-normalized forms are never rendered as sacred/source text. Generative AI is never the authority for Quran text, Hadith text, numbering or grades.

No account is required for core reading, search or learning. Reading/learning history is local by default. External AI receives only evidence explicitly selected/exported by the user.

Reading is primary; drills support it. Passive visibility is not successful recall. Rare-word review is triggered by personal struggle plus forgetting risk and exposure scarcity, not rarity alone.
